Before you start: nothing in this station has run on hardware yet. The receiver is the C5's Wi-Fi PHY driven through undocumented calls that the C5VRX project proved on ESP-IDF 6.0; this firmware re-implements them on the fleet's Arduino/ESP-IDF 5.5 toolchain. The procedure on sheets 6–8 is ordered so that each stage proves the next one is worth doing: receiver alive, channel coverage, selectivity, video (NTSC then PAL), sensitivity against an RX5808 station, then the switch and the pattern. Two rules that are easy to break: the eight I/Q lane pads (D0, D1, D3, D10 and the GPIO2–5 back pads) must stay unconnected, and every flash starts with a full erase. Full BOM on sheet 5.

1 · Full block diagram

Four patches feed an SP4T switch whose common port goes straight into the XIAO's U.FL antenna connector. Inside the C5 the Wi-Fi PHY is held receive-only on the FPV frequency; its raw I/Q leaves on the modem's diagnostic bus, travels out through eight GPIO pads and back into the PARLIO peripheral, and the firmware measures power, FM coherence and horizontal sync on 16 KiB windows. The XIAO's 3V3 regulator feeds the switch; the 5 V rail feeds the XIAO and the Heltec.

GPIO numbers are from the Arduino core's XIAO_ESP32C5 variant, used through config.h; wire by the D-labels on the silkscreen. The variant's D4 = GPIO23 / D5 = GPIO24 is now corroborated: C5VRX measured their resistor DAC on the XIAO's D4–D9 pads as GPIO 23, 24, 11, 12, 8, 9. The I/Q lanes are GPIO numbers (1, 0, 2, 7, 10, 5, 3, 4 in PARLIO data order), the set C5VRX proved on hardware. They need real pads: each MODEM_DIAG bit is driven out through the pad and read back by PARLIO from the same pad, so a load on the pad corrupts the samples.

2 · Sector layout and receiver

Top view. Each patch covers about 70°, so an emitter always sits inside one sector and near the edge of a neighbour. Unchanged from v2.

Method

Amplitude comparison. Take the strongest sector k and its two neighbours; with powers in dB the bearing is

bearing = az[k] + K · (P[k+1] − P[k−1])

K, in degrees per dB, comes from the measured patch pattern. A bench sweep of a VTX around the box every 15° fills the lookup table. Report bearing_deg and bearing_sigma_deg; sigma grows when the two neighbours are nearly equal, or the peak is weak.

Per-channel scan loop

rf_tune(channel); delay(8 ms)
for sector in N,E,S,W:
    select(sector); wait 200 µs
    gain = 62
    for w in 1..3 (1 on a quiet channel):
        capture 16 KiB @ 40 MS/s     (0.4 ms)
        metrics                       (~2 ms)
        if clip > 3 %: gain -= 12; retake
    level = min(windows); q = median
hit = level[best] >= 8 dB and q[best] >= 40 %

Sweep ≈ 40 × (10 + 4 × 8 ms) ≈ 1.7 s at the full window count. The gain steps keep strong carriers unclipped and weak ones at full gain; the minimum over windows drops Wi-Fi bursts.

3 · Video check and serial output

Video check

On the strongest due peaks after the sweep: hold the channel and the sector, demodulate 8 windows 5 ms apart (phase step between samples = instantaneous frequency), find sync-tip pulses 3.2–7.2 µs wide below a threshold set from the window's own percentiles, and measure the spacing of consecutive pulses.

Line periodLine rateVerdictMeaning
64.0 µs (2560 samples)15 625 HzPALreal analog video carrier
63.56 µs (2542 samples)15 734 HzNTSCreal analog video carrier
sync-shaped pulses, no repeating period—nonenot video-shaped: noise, Wi-Fi, an unmodulated carrier
swing under 4 units/sample—noneno FM deviation on this channel

A window counts with ≥ 2 consecutive periods and a sync score ≥ 70 (width closeness 0–40 + period closeness 0–60); the carrier is video when ≥ 3 of 8 windows count. sync_q is the share of windows that counted. The field rate is not measured (a 410 µs window never sees a vertical interval): field_hz is the nominal 50/60 of the detected standard. The fingerprint fp is <video>/<sync_hz>/<freq_peak>, e.g. NTSC/15736/5734, where freq_peak is the tuned frequency plus the measured carrier offset — no synthesizer sweep needed.

Expected serial output

Boot line on USB at 115200. "rf":true is the PHY receive-only and the PARLIO reader running; false is followed by an {"info":"error",…} line naming the failed call. The bench console (?, h, s, g, v, x) prints {"info":"bench",…} lines with level_db, gain, q_phase, p_mean, clip, cfo_khz, mod, noise, phy_rssi, nf_dbm.

5 · Bench procedure — is the receiver alive?

Work top to bottom on a bare XIAO with the stock antenna on the U.FL; the switch and patches come in stage 6. Do not move to the next stage until the current one gives the stated result. Console commands are typed into the serial monitor and Enter-terminated: ? status, h R3/h 5732 hold, s 0–3 sector, g 30/g a gain, v video check, x resume.

Stage 0 — Flash and boot
pio run -e seeed_xiao_esp32c5 --target erase, then --target upload. A full erase precedes every flash: stale PHY calibration data left C5VRX's receiver deaf after reflashes. Boot mode if it will not connect: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT.
Monitor at 115200. Expected: {"info":"c5phy v3 station ready",…,"rf":true}. If "rf":false, copy the {"info":"error"} line here:
? twice, 60 s apart. Expected: captures climbing (hundreds per second while scanning), cap_err 0. Measured: captures/min cap_err
Stage 1 — Receiver alive
No VTX on. h A1. Expected: level_db 0 ± 2, p_mean ≈ 2, q_phase under 15, gain 62, clip 0. Measured: level_db p_mean q_phase
Note the PHY's own no-signal figures: phy_rssi dBm, nf_dbm dBm. p_mean far from 2 means RF_NOISE_POWER is not this board's value — write it down for the calibration.
Key a VTX on A1 (5865 MHz) a few metres away. Expected: level_db up by ≥ 30 dB, gain stepping below 62, q_phase ≥ 50, mod 1, noise 0, cfo_khz within ±2000. Measured: level_db gain q_phase cfo
Move the VTX away or unkey it. Expected: the readings return to the no-signal values on the next line. level_db rising without q_phase rising means power but no FM coherence: an off-channel carrier (check cfo_khz) or scrambled lane order.

x to resume scanning; wait for the first heartbeat (60 s). Expected: tune_fail 0, channels 40. Measured: tune_fail

VTX on R3 (5732), scanning. Expected: a detection line for R3 within two sweeps; B1 (5733) and F1 (5740) may hit too and PEAK_PICK folds them into one. Channels reported:
Expected: no hit on any channel more than 40 MHz away. If one appears, h it and note q_phase (a hit needs ≥ 40) and cfo_khz .

Hold the FPV channel nearest a live 5 GHz access point (A-band channels 5745–5825 overlap Wi-Fi 149–165). Expected: raised level_db but q_phase under 40 and no hit while scanning. Measured: level_db q_phase

Known level into the U.FL on a held channel. The slope should be 1 dB/dB; the two-point line sets RSSI_CAL_DBM_AT_NOISE and RSSI_CAL_SLOPE in config.h. Where the firmware has stepped the gain down, level_db already includes the (62 − gain) term.

Reading the numbers. level_db is dB above the noise reference (mean I²+Q² of 2.0 at gain 62); rssi_dbm = RSSI_CAL_DBM_AT_NOISE + level_db × RSSI_CAL_SLOPE + RSSI_CAL_OFFSET_DB. The default −95 dBm is kTB over ~20 MHz plus a ~6 dB noise figure, not a measurement. A slope far from 1 dB/dB, or a floor much worse than the RX5808's, is a real result — record it, do not tune the constants to hide it.

7 · Bench procedure — switch, pattern, field, power

Stage 6 — RF switch and pattern
Eval board powered from 3V3, one patch on RF1, common port → XIAO U.FL, control lines from D2/D8/D9 through 2 kΩ. Drive each combination and record which RF port goes through. Write the truth table here (the firmware's V1 is D2):
V1V2V3 = 000 → RF__    100 → RF__    010 → RF__    110 → RF__   → SECTOR_SWITCH_TABLE
VTX on, hold its channel, s 0 … s 3. Expected: level_db follows the sector the patch is on and settles within one line. Insertion loss against the patch wired direct to the U.FL: dB (expect ≈ 0.5)
Mount all four patches on the box, VTX at 30 m on a known bearing, clear line of sight. Rotate the box in 15° steps through a full turn, logging all four sectors values at each step.
Derive K in degrees per dB from the neighbour difference. Value: °/dB (default 3.0). Save the sweep to docs/ as the station's bearing calibration.
Stage 7 — Field acceptance
Two v3 stations 300–500 m apart, both positions set in the mapper, both named to match their Meshtastic nodes, D4/D5 wired to the Heltecs.
Walk a VTX between them on ten marked points. Pass: the 90 % circle contains the true position on at least 9 of 10. Score: / 10
Pass: video reads NTSC or PAL on every report for a live camera, never none; the mesh copy arrives with fp.
Leave both stations on solar for 72 h. Pass: no brownout, heartbeat gap never exceeds 5 min. Measured station current at 5 V: mA (expect ≈ 200)
